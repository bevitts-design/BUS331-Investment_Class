FactSet Bond Fund Research

Explore six bond ETFs and three bond mutual funds. Use FactSet to identify what each fund invests in, its costs and income, its competitors, and its performance.

Start with the Excel worksheet

  1. Download the student Excel worksheet attached to this assignment and open it in Excel.
  2. Enter your name and save your copy as LastName_FirstName_FactSet_Bond_Funds.xlsx.
  3. Complete the yellow response cells on Fund comparison. Use the FactSet hints tab for additional navigation help.

No calculations are required. Copy the information displayed in FactSet. If a field or report is unavailable, enter N/A. Leave unused additional-statistic rows blank.

Work through the FactSet screens in order

Choose one fund, complete the six sections below, and then repeat for the next fund. Keep the same fund selected as you change screens. Report names and field availability may vary by FactSet version; use report search or the worksheet’s hints if needed.

1. Fund Overview

Record these seven items:

  • Asset Class, Category, and Focus: copy the exact classification labels.
  • Net asset value (NAV): record the value in USD per share.
  • Assets under management (AUM): include the displayed unit, such as millions or billions.
  • Net expense ratio: use the net figure when both net and gross are shown.
  • 30-day SEC yield: use the field with that exact label.

Hint: Check the fund summary, fees, and yield panels. If a summary field is missing, try Fund Profile or ETF Snapshot, as appropriate. Dividend yield and SEC yield are different measures.

2. Comp Analysis

Identify two competitors shown in the fund’s comparison list. Record each competitor’s full name and ticker. Choose two different funds and exclude the fund you are currently researching.

3. Holding/Holder Summary

Locate Fund All Holding Statistics and record:

  • Effective duration in years.
  • Number of holdings from the summary count.
  • Additional statistics displayed in the panel that you have not already entered elsewhere. For each, enter its exact label, value, and unit in one cell.

Use the eight additional-statistic rows as needed and the continuation row if more remain. Leave unused rows blank. Do not repeat a field already recorded. Different measures remain separate: effective duration differs from maturity, and yield to maturity differs from SEC yield.

4. Ownership/Company Summary

Open Ownership, then Company Summary. Find the investor-type breakdown. Record the top three investor types, ordered from largest to smallest displayed ownership percentage, and the percentage for each.

Use investor-type categories rather than individual holder names. This section describes who owns the fund; the holdings section describes what the fund owns. If the breakdown is unavailable, enter N/A.

5. Price Summary

  • For ETFs: record the latest market price in USD per share and average daily trading volume. Include any displayed volume unit or averaging-period label in the same answer cell.
  • For the three mutual funds: enter N/A for market price and trading volume. Their daily NAV is recorded in Fund Overview.

Hint: Look for Last Price or Closing Price and Average Volume. Today’s Volume is different from average daily volume.

6. Fund Performance

Open the Total Returns table and record:

  • Year-to-date total return.
  • Trailing 1-year total return.

Use the same return basis for both figures. Mutual funds use NAV total return. For ETFs, use a consistent NAV or market-price total-return table. Total return includes distributions; a price change does not. A previous calendar-year return is different from a trailing 1-year return.
