# Phase 2 · Scenario Reveal Control Packet

Keep these materials private until each team has submitted its locked baseline snapshot. A reveal packet must force the team to confront at least one meaningful trade-off under an approved client constraint; it must not provide the solution.

## Before release

- Confirm the baseline allocation, IPS scorecard, and initial stress result were submitted in Canvas.
- Select the client goal or constraint that the adverse event will test.
- Calibrate the packet so the submitted baseline breaches at least one tripwire, objective, or constraint.

## Each packet must state

1. Packet ID and release date.
2. Assigned team and named client.
3. The adverse event and exact inputs to enter in the stress workbook.
4. The named goal, constraint, or tripwire being tested.
5. Required response: document the breach, revise holdings/weights, state the trade-off, and re-test the whole IPS.

## Do not include

- A recommended security, allocation, hedge, or trade.
- A completed workbook or solved remediation.
- Any FactSet export or licensed evidence outside the approved Canvas channel.
