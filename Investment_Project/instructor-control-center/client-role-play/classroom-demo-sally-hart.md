# Instructor-Led Demonstration · Sally Hart

Use this 15-minute model interview before teams begin their assigned cases. Sally Hart is a fictional practice client and is not an assigned client.

## Before class

Give one volunteer only the sealed client card below. Ask the rest of the class to listen for: established facts, information gaps, and one guardrail the committee should carry into an IPS.

## Facilitation sequence

1. **Set the task (1 minute):** “We are discovering, not recommending. Ask neutral questions; do not name investments.”
2. **Interview (7 minutes):** Invite three to five student questions. Pause after two answers and ask the class what has been established versus assumed.
3. **Translate (4 minutes):** Record the tuition reserve, long-horizon goal, taxable-account status, and one unresolved item in a visible sample Decision Log.
4. **Debrief (3 minutes):** Explain that a long horizon does not remove a known three-year cash need. The committee must protect the tuition need before pursuing long-term growth.

---

## Sealed client card — give only to the volunteer

**You are Sally Hart, a fictional practice client.** Be candid and thoughtful. Do not recommend investments or supply facts that are not listed.

### Reveal only when asked

- You have **$500,000** in hypothetical assets for this exercise.
- A **$40,000 tuition payment is due in three years**.
- A separate **$25,000 emergency reserve is outside** the modeled portfolio.
- Your primary long-term horizon is **25 years**.
- The classroom exercise uses a **5.5% nominal planning target** and a **12.0% maximum one-year drawdown**.
- The account is taxable.

### Say this if needed

- “That is not established in my brief.”
- “I want the tuition money to be there when it is needed.”
- “I understand there is a longer-term goal too, but I do not want the nearer goal ignored.”

### Do not add

Do not invent family details, tax bracket, existing holdings, product preferences, risk score, or the amount/timing of any other cash need.
