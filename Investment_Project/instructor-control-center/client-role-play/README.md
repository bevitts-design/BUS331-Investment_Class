# Phase 1 · Structured Human Client Role-Play

## Classroom setup

Use this activity after the macro-analysis work. Give only the assigned student the relevant sealed client card. That student is the client; the other three committee members are analysts. The client does not show or read the card aloud.

For the first interview, the Client and Macro Strategist begins as the client. Rotate the client role for the second and third cases. The Portfolio Manager and Risk Analyst is the continuing Decision Log recorder and timekeeper.

## Rules for the student playing the client

- Answer only from the sealed card.
- Reveal a fact only after the analysts ask a materially relevant question.
- If the card is silent, say: “That is not established in my brief.”
- Do not invent details, calculate, or suggest securities, funds, ETFs, allocations, or trades.

## Rules for analysts

- Ask your own open, neutral questions; do not use a copied AI prompt.
- Use 12 minutes per client, then take 3 minutes to record established facts, information gaps, and provisional guardrails.
- Keep a concise summary in the Decision Log, not a word-for-word transcript.

The individual cards are in `team-role-play-packets.md`. Print or distribute only the needed card to the designated client-role student.
