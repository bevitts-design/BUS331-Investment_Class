BUS331 · EQUITY RESEARCH LAB
Workflow, saving and research conventions

Suggested 75 minutes: role and identities 10 · candidate/alternative evidence 30 · valuation and scenarios 20 · recommendation and peer challenge 15. Finish verification after class. ETF sector/holdings research may need additional time.

Keep licensed FactSet observations and completed reports in private Canvas submissions. Browser storage is local and may be cleared; save the JSON research file for portable backup. Loading replaces the current record only after confirmation. There is no shared account, automatic submission or live quote feed.

ETF sector weights include all eleven equity sectors plus cash and other/unclassified. Do not silently normalize away non-equity residuals. Sector percentages refer to the ETF, while proposed position size refers to the total portfolio. NAV is not intrinsic value. Use reported aggregate ETF valuation; do not average holding P/Es. Historical return and yield are not expected return.
